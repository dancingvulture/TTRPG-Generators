# first change
add squiggly tail to one corner or edge at bottom, pointing left | add squiggly tail to one corner or edge at bottom, poitning right | add squiggly tail to one corner or edge at top, pointing left | add squiggly tail to one corner or edge at top, pointing right | mirror image, or, if this results in no change, connect two random points in the basic form with a straight line | put a *basic form* below it, touching, upside down | put a *basic form* next to it, touching | put an identical figure below it, touching, upside down | put an identical figure next to it, touching | turn on side, clockwise | turn on side, counterclockwise | turn upside down, or, if this results in no change, connect two random points in the basic form with a curved line
