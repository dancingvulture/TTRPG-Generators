# first change
mirror image, or, if this results in no change, connect two random points in the basic form with a straight line | add squiggly tail to one corner or edge at bottom, pointing left | add squiggly tail to one corner or edge at bottom, poitning right | add squiggly tail to one corner or edge at top, pointing left | add squiggly tail to one corner or edge at top, pointing right | put a *basic form* below it, touching, upside down | put a *basic form* next to it, touching | put an identical figure below it, touching, upside down | put an identical figure next to it, touching | turn on side, clockwise | turn on side, counterclockwise | turn upside down, or, if this results in no change, connect two random points in the basic form with a curved line

# second change
put a dot to the left of it | put a dot to the right of it | put a dot above it | put a dot below it | put a horizontal line above | put a diagonal line above | put a curved line above | attach a small circle to the top | attach a small circle to the bottom | attach a small circle to the left | attach a small circle to the right | place a small circle to the top | place a small circle to the bottom | place a small circle to the left | place a small circle to the right | duplicate the figure, upside down, directly below the figure | duplicate the figure, upside down, directly above the figure | duplicate the figure, upside down, directly to the left of the figure | duplicate the figure, upside down, directly to the right of the figure | start from one of the figures points or ends, and draw a spiraling line startin from that point | put a wavy line beneath the figure | put a wavy line above the figure | put a wavy line to the left of the figure | put a wavy line to the right of the figure
